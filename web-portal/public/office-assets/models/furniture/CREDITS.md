# Office furniture models — credits

These `.glb` furniture models are from **Kenney's Furniture Kit**, released under
**CC0 1.0 (public domain)** — https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit

No attribution is legally required for CC0 assets, but it is provided here as a
courtesy. The models are used by the isometric virtual office
(`web-portal/src/office/retro/`) and loaded at runtime via drei `useGLTF`.
